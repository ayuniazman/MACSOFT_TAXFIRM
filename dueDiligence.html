Due Diligence

Tax-focused due diligence to help businesses identify tax exposures, unpaid liabilities and hidden risks before completing a transaction.

What It Is

Understanding Tax Due Diligence

Tax due diligence is a detailed review of a target company's tax position before a merger, acquisition or investment. It helps buyers understand the real tax cost of a deal.

In Malaysia, LHDN can review past tax filings up to 5 years back (or longer if fraud is suspected). A careful due diligence review highlights issues before they become a problem after the deal closes.

Scope of Review

What We Typically Examine

Each engagement is tailored to the transaction, but key areas include the following.

Income Tax Compliance

Review of filed returns, payments and any outstanding liabilities with LHDN.

Withholding Tax

Check that WHT was deducted and remitted correctly on cross-border payments.

Transfer Pricing

Review related-party transactions and documentation for compliance with TP rules.

SST

Review of Sales and Service Tax registration, reporting and payments.

Director Accounts

Review loans or advances to directors that may attract tax under Section 140B.

Tax Incentives

Confirm eligibility and compliance with any tax incentives previously claimed.

Why It Matters

Protecting Buyers Before the Deal Closes

Tax issues in a target company can become the buyer's problem after completion. Proper due diligence gives clarity and negotiating leverage.

  • Spot hidden liabilities: Identify unpaid taxes or exposures before signing.
  • Adjust the price: Use findings to renegotiate price or request indemnities.
  • Plan the structure: Choose share vs asset purchase with clear tax impact.
  • Reduce post-deal surprises: Avoid unexpected LHDN audits or penalties after closing.
