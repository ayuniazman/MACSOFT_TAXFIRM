Tax Planning

Introduction

Why plan your income tax?

Malaysian tax law places many duties on taxpayers, but it also offers reliefs and incentives that reduce what you pay. Business owners who want to keep more of their income benefit from proper planning.

The saving may look small in a single year, but over the long term it adds up, and tax bills can reach millions of ringgit. Paying a modest fee for a good tax expert is an easy trade.

Tips

8 tips for income tax saving

Simple habits that can lower your tax legally.

01

Claim every deduction

Deductions lower your taxable income, so use every relief you can. Keep good records for any IRBM audit.

02

Submit and pay on time

Late submission or payment can bring heavy penalties. Keep your records ready.

03

Follow the Acts

IRBM administers six direct tax Acts and issues public rulings. Following them avoids trouble.

04

Apply for incentives

Government incentives can reduce a company's tax. See the list below.

05

Ask a tax consultant

An agent saves time and may find deductions or offsets you missed. The fee is deductible next year.

06

Be charitable

Donations are deducted from taxable income, so you get back a percentage. Keep the official receipt.

07

Earn tax-free income

Some income is not taxed, such as bank deposits, bonds, shares and certain employee benefits like childcare allowance.

Tax avoidance is legal. Tax evasion is not.

Evasion means cutting tax through deceit, subterfuge or concealment. What separates it from avoidance is usually IRBM finding fraudulent intent. Areas it commonly focuses on include:

  • Failing to report substantial income
  • Fictitious or improper deductions with no proof
  • Accounting irregularities, such as poor records or returns that do not match the financial statements

Evasion is prohibited in Malaysia and can bring heavy penalties, imprisonment or both. We will not help any client evade tax. Every taxpayer should pay tax on their chargeable income under the Income Tax Act 1967.
