Tax Planning

Introduction

Why plan your income tax?

Malaysian tax law places many duties on taxpayers, but it also offers reliefs and incentives that reduce what you pay. Business owners who want to keep more of their income benefit from proper planning.

Tax planning involves strategizing to reduce the amount of business tax for the current or future years. It does not entail evading tax payments or concealing income; rather, it involves utilizing appropriate, lawful methods—in compliance with established regulations—to lower one's tax liability.

8 Strategies For Tax Planning

Simple habits that can lower your tax legally.

01

Pick the right business structure

A sole proprietorship and a company are taxed in a similar way, but they differ in tax rate, allowed expenses and how assets are owned. Online sellers, direct-selling agents and people earning commission usually need to register a business and keep accounts.

02

Keep clean financial statements

Show income against expenses clearly. Every cost and expense should have a supporting document, and your gross profit should stay consistent from year to year.

03

Claim only allowed expenses

An expense is deductible when it is incurred wholly and exclusively to earn business income (Section 33(1)), Travel, staff pay, commissions and part of home-office costs can qualify. Entertainment has special conditions.

04

Watch the risky expenses

Common problems are missing documents, payments to third parties, mistakes in staff paperwork, and expenses involving a spouse or relatives. Organised documents lower your audit risk.

05

Manage your assests wisely

The timing of a purchase, whose name the asset is under, leasing versus a loan or hire purchase, and how you dipose of it can all change your tax. Decide for business reasons first.

06

Give to approved causes

Gifts to the government, approved institutions, approved sports activities and national-interest projects can be deducted, usually up to a limit. Keep the official receipt.

07

Claim every relief

Reliefs cover you, your spouse, children and parents, plus education, medical care, insurance, retirement savings, lifestyle spending and more. The list and limits change often, so check the current year.

08

Use zakat to reduce tax

Zakat paid to the state religious council or zakat centre reduces the tax you owe, after you tax is calculated. The receipt date decides the year it counts for.

Tax planning is legal. Tax evasion is not.

Evasion means cutting tax through deceit, subterfuge or concealment. What separates it from planning is usually IRBM finding fraudulent intent. Areas it commonly focuses on include:

  • Failing to report substantial income
  • Fictitious or improper deductions with no proof
  • Accounting irregularities, such as poor records or returns that do not match the financial statements

Evasion is prohibited in Malaysia and can bring heavy penalties, imprisonment or both. We will not help any client evade tax. Every taxpayer should pay tax on their chargeable income under the Income Tax Act 1967.
