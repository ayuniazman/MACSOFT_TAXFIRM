ACCOUNTING SERVICES

ACCOUNTING THAT SUPPORTS YOUR BUSINESS

More Than Just Keeping Records

Accurate financial information is essential to running a successful business. Without organised accounting records, it can be difficult to monitor expenses, understand profitability and keep track of financial obligations.

At W&C, we aim to provide accounting support that helps businesses maintain reliable financial records and gain a clearer understanding of their financial performance. From organising transactions to preparing financial reports, our proposed service offering is designed around your business requirements.

WHAT WE DO

Accounting Solutions for Your Business Needs

Every business has different accounting requirements. Explore the areas of accounting support that may help you keep your financial information organised.

01

Bookkeeping Services

Maintaining accurate and up-to-date records of your business transactions.

Depending on the agreed scope, bookkeeping may include:

  • Recording sales, purchases and expenses
  • Maintaining general ledger records
  • Organising receipts and supporting documents
  • Recording relevant journal entries
How it helps

Better organised financial records and a more reliable basis for financial reporting.

02

Financial Statement Preparation

Preparing financial statements that provide an overview of your business's financial position and performance.

Depending on reporting requirements, this may include:

  • Statement of Profit or Loss
  • Statement of Financial Position
  • Statement of Cash Flows, where applicable
  • Supporting financial schedules
How it helps

A clearer picture of business performance, assets, liabilities and financial position.

03

Account Reconciliation

Reviewing and matching financial records to help ensure account balances are consistent and accurate.

Reconciliation activities may include:

  • Bank reconciliation
  • Accounts payable reconciliation
  • Accounts receivable reconciliation
  • General ledger reconciliation
How it helps

Helps identify discrepancies, minimise recording errors and improve the reliability of financial information.

04

Accounts Payable and Receivable Management

Helping businesses monitor money owed to suppliers and payments due from customers.

Support may include:

  • Recording supplier and customer invoices
  • Monitoring outstanding payments
  • Tracking payment transactions
  • Maintaining payable and receivable records
How it helps

Better visibility over outstanding balances and more organised payment and collection records.

05

Management Accounting and Reporting

Providing periodic accounting information and reports to help business owners monitor their operations.

Reports may include:

  • Monthly or quarterly management reports
  • Income and expense summaries
  • Profitability reports
  • Financial performance summaries
How it helps

Better understanding of financial performance and more informed operational decisions.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions About Our Accounting Services?

Find answers to common questions about accounting support.

Ask Us a Question →
What are accounting services?

Accounting services involve recording, organising, reviewing and reporting a business's financial transactions. They help businesses maintain financial records and understand their financial position.

What is the difference between bookkeeping and accounting?

Bookkeeping primarily involves recording daily financial transactions, while accounting involves reviewing, organising, analysing and reporting financial information.

Who can benefit from accounting services?

Startups, SMEs and established businesses can benefit, particularly those that need assistance maintaining financial records and preparing reports.

What documents may I need to provide?

Depending on the agreed scope, documents may include bank statements, sales and purchase invoices, receipts, payment records and previous accounting reports.

How frequently should accounting records be updated?

This depends on transaction volume and reporting requirements. Records may be maintained monthly, quarterly or according to an agreed schedule.

Does accounting service include tax filing or auditing?

Accounting, tax compliance and statutory audit services are distinct professional scopes. Contact W&C to discuss the services relevant to your business.
