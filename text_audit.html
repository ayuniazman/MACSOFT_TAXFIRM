Income Tax Audit

Know what an IRB audit involves, which type you may face, and how to prepare.

Introduction

Every company can expect an audit

Every company is subject to an IRB income tax audit every 3 to 5 years.

The audit examines your business records and financial affairs. It confirms that the right amount of income was declared, and that the right amount of tax was calculated and paid under income tax law and regulations.

See the two audit types →
Types

Two types of income tax audit

01

Desk audit

You receive a formal letter listing the documents to be examined and the grace period to prepare and submit them to the IRB branch.

  • Done at the IRB office
  • You can apply for more time if the period is too short
  • The officer in charge tells you what was found in the documents
  • Some desk audit cases can be referred for field audit
02

Field audit

You receive a formal letter with the date and time the IRB officers will visit your office or house, and the extent of the audit.

  • Done at your office or house
  • The letter states the Year of Assessment to be examined
  • You will be interviewed by the IRB officer
  • Prepare every document the officer asks for
