The big picture

Two broad categories.
Different responsibilities.

Malaysia’s tax framework includes direct and indirect taxes. The applicable tax, rate and filing obligations depend on the taxpayer, transaction and current rules.

01 / DIRECT TAXES↗

Tax linked to income, gains or profits.

Direct taxes are generally imposed on a person or entity based on income, profits or certain gains.

Income taxReal Property Gains TaxPetroleum income tax
02 / INDIRECT TAXES↗

Tax connected to goods, services and transactions.

Indirect taxes may apply when goods or services are supplied, imported, manufactured or when certain legal instruments are executed.

Sales & Service TaxCustoms & excise dutiesStamp duty

▤DIRECT TAX

Income tax

Income tax generally applies to taxable income earned by individuals and entities. The calculation and filing obligations depend on factors such as residency, income source, business structure and available reliefs or deductions.
