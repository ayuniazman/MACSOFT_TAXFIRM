Understanding Virtual CFO

What is a
Virtual CFO?

A Virtual Chief Financial Officer (CFO) provides businesses with access to professional financial management and strategic guidance without the commitment of employing a full-time CFO.

W&C's Virtual CFO service is designed to help businesses gain a clearer understanding of their financial position, strengthen financial planning and make informed decisions that support their business objectives.

From monitoring financial performance and managing cash flow to reviewing financial plans, businesses can benefit from a more structured approach to managing their finances and planning ahead.

What is a Virtual CFO?

A Virtual CFO is an outsourced financial professional who provides financial management, planning and strategic guidance to a business without requiring the business to employ a full-time CFO.

How is a Virtual CFO different from an accountant?

An accountant generally focuses on accounting records, financial processes and reporting. A Virtual CFO focuses more broadly on financial strategy, forecasting, performance analysis and business decision support. The responsibilities may overlap depending on the agreed scope of work.

Who can benefit from Virtual CFO services?

Startups, small and medium-sized enterprises and established businesses may benefit when they need additional financial expertise, clearer financial reporting or support with financial planning and business decisions.

Does a Virtual CFO replace an in-house CFO?

Not necessarily. Virtual CFO support can be an alternative for businesses that do not require a full-time CFO, or it can supplement an existing finance team where additional expertise is needed.

Are Virtual CFO services related to tax planning?

Financial decisions can have tax implications. Virtual CFO support may consider relevant tax factors as part of wider financial planning. Specific tax compliance and advisory matters can be addressed through the firm's separate tax services.

Can the services be tailored to my business?

The scope and working arrangement can be discussed according to your business's financial requirements, objectives and operational needs.
