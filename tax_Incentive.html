Income Tax Incentive

Malaysia offers various tax incentives to attract foreign and local investments, specifically in manufacturing, agriculture, tourism, approved services sectors, R&D, training, and environmental protection activities

Introduction

Overview of Tax Incentives

Malaysia’s tax incentives are designed to direct investment toward priority sectors, technologies, and regions by reducing the effective corporate tax burden through

income exemptions, capital allowances, and enhanced deductions, thereby enabling reinvestment in growth, innovation, and workforce development.

Major Incentives

Main Types of Incentives

01

Pioneer Status

A partial or full exemption from income tax on the profit of a promoted business.

  • Standard: 70% of statutory income is exempt for 5 years, so tax is paid on the other 30%
  • Some projects: 100% exemption for 5 or 10 years
  • The period starts on Production Day: when production reaches 30% of capacity, or the first invoice for a service company
  • Unused capital allowances can be carried forward to the income after the pioneer period
  • Losses can be carried forward for up to 7 consecutive years after the period ends
02

Investment Tax Allowance (ITA)

An alternative to Pioneer Status, built for projects with large capital spending.

  • A common rate is 60% of qualifying capital spending, such as factory, plant, machinery and equipment for the approved project
  • The spending counts if it is incurred within 5 years of the first qualifying spending
  • The allowance is set against statutory income each year
  • Any unused allowance carries forward until it is fully used
  • Rates and conditions differ by sector and project
03

Infrastructure Allowance

An allowance for qualifying spending on infrastructure for an approved project.

  • For capital spending to expand, modernise, automate or diversify an existing manufacturing business or approved agricultural project
  • Given for 15 years from the first year of claim
  • 60% of qualifying capital expenditure, used against 70% of statutory income, with the other 30% taxed at the prevailing rate
  • The 70% limit does not apply to projects that reach the productivity level set by the Minister of Finance
  • The allowance is withdrawn if the asset is sold within five years
04

Industrial Adjustment Allowance

An allowance to help existing companies cope with changes by spending on qualifying assets.

  • Allowance of 10% to 100% of the increase in exports
  • Prescribed conditions must be met
  • Deductible against up to 70% of statutory income
