Income Tax Incentive

Introduction

Incentives for every size of company

Small and medium sized companies, as well as large and multinational companies, can use the many tax incentives offered in Malaysia.

The incentives vary by industry and change with the annual Budgets announced by the Government.

Major incentives

Four incentives to know

01

Pioneer Status

For companies engaged in promoted activities or producing promoted products.

  • Partial exemption from income tax for five to ten years
  • The company pays tax on 30% of its statutory income
  • The period starts on Production Day, when production reaches 30% of capacity
  • Unabsorbed capital allowances and accumulated losses from the pioneer period carry forward against post-pioneer income
02

Investment Tax Allowance (ITA)

An alternative to Pioneer Status. A company cannot have both.

  • 60% allowance on qualifying capital expenditure (factory, plant, machinery or equipment for the approved project)
  • The spending must be incurred within five years of the first qualifying expenditure
  • The allowance offsets up to 70% of statutory income each year
  • Unused allowance carries forward until fully used
  • The other 30% is taxed at the prevailing company tax rate
03

Reinvestment Allowance

Granted under Schedule 7A of the Income Tax Act 1967 to a resident company operating for at least 36 months.

  • For capital spending to expand, modernise, automate or diversify an existing manufacturing business or approved agricultural project
  • Given for 15 years from the first year of claim
  • 60% of qualifying capital expenditure, used against 70% of statutory income, with the other 30% taxed at the prevailing rate
  • The 70% limit does not apply to projects that reach the productivity level set by the Minister of Finance
  • The allowance is withdrawn if the asset is sold within five years
04

Allowance for Increased Exports

For a resident company in manufacturing, agriculture or services that exports its products, produce or services.

  • Allowance of 10% to 100% of the increase in exports
  • Prescribed conditions must be met
  • Deductible against up to 70% of statutory income
