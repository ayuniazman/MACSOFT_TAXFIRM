Income Tax Audit

An official examination of tax returns and financial records to verify compliance with tax laws.

Introduction

What is a Tax Audit?

In Malaysia, the tax audit process conducted by Inland Revenue Board of Malaysia (IRBM) plays a vital role in ensuring the integrity of the Self-Assessment System (SAS).

A tax audit is the process where LHDN reviews and verifies a taxpayer's income, expenses, and tax declarations to ensure that correct taxes have been paid. It does not imply wrongdoing, but serves as a check to enhance compliance.

Objectives of Tax Audit

01

Promote Tax Compliance

Encourage taxpayers to comply voluntarily with the Income Tax Act 1967 and other relevant tax legislation.

02

Educate Taxpayers

Raise awareness and help taxpayers and employers understand their rights and responsibilities.

03

Prevent Tax Leakage

Identify errors, omissions and non-compliance through the proper enforcement of tax laws.

04

Ensure Accurate Reporting

Verify that income is properly reported and the correct amount of tax is paid to the government.

Types of Tax Audit

01

Income Tax Audit

Examines the income, expenses, deductions and tax declarations of individuals, companies and businesses to verify their tax compliance.

02

Withholding Tax Audit

Reviews payments made to non-residents and verifies whether the required withholding tax has been deducted and remitted to IRBM.

03

Capital Gains Tax Audit

Examines relevant disposals of capital assets and verifies the reporting and payment of capital gains tax where applicable.

04

Labuan Business Activity Audit

Reviews Labuan business activities and relevant records to verify compliance with Labuan tax requirements and eligibility for applicable tax treatment.

05

Employer Audit

Examines employer responsibilities, including employee remuneration records, Monthly Tax Deduction (PCB), CP38 deductions and required employee information reporting.

Methods of Audit Tax

01

Desk Audit

A desk audit is a document-based examination conducted at an IRBM office. It generally focuses on specific information declared in a taxpayer's income tax return.

  • Conducted at an IRBM office
  • Usually involves correspondence with the taxpayer
  • Reviews income, expenses and deductions
  • May require supporting documents and explanations
02

Field Audit

A field audit involves a more detailed examination of a taxpayer's business records and financial information. It may include a visit to the taxpayer's business premises.

  • May involve an on-site visit
  • Examines accounting and financial records
  • May include interviews with relevant personnel
  • Requires supporting documents and explanations

How Does a Tax Audit Work?

01

Selection for Audit

Cases may be selected through risk assessment, information from third parties, identified compliance issues or other selection methods.

Selection for an audit does not automatically indicate wrongdoing.

02

Audit Notification

IRBM provides notification of the audit and relevant details, which may include:

  • Type of audit
  • Year of assessment under review
  • Documents required
  • Audit officer's contact details
03

Document Submission

The taxpayer prepares and submits the requested documents within the timeframe stated by IRBM.

  • Income tax returns
  • Financial statements
  • Invoices and receipts
  • Accounting and bank records
04

Audit Examination

IRBM officers review the submitted information and compare it with the taxpayer's tax declarations. Further explanations may be requested.

05

Audit Findings

IRBM communicates the findings and any proposed adjustments. The taxpayer may be given an opportunity to provide explanations or raise objections according to the applicable framework.

06

Finalisation and Assessment

After considering the taxpayer's response, IRBM determines the outcome. This may result in an additional assessment or no adjustment, depending on the findings.

07

Audit Closure

Once the audit matters have been resolved and the necessary procedures completed, the audit case is closed.
